# 18 November 2026 in one image

A newspaper front page — "One Day on Earth" — summing up Wednesday
18 November 2026 in six stories, drawn by a pure-Python (standard
library only) generator, `main.py`. It writes a deterministic
1024×1024 PNG to `/output/entry.png`: the same bytes on every run,
no network, no clock, no randomness.

## The six stories

1. **The Gulf** — US jets strike an Iranian missile site on Qeshm
   Island; Iran answers with drones at US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain.
2. **Shenzhen** — Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin meet at the APEC summit,
   a day ahead of the trilateral with Donald Trump.
3. **Alaska** — Mary Peltola wins the last Senate race; Democrats take
   the Senate 56–44.
4. **Tech** — Nvidia doubles revenue to $115.8 billion; the board will
   not bring Jensen Huang back as CEO.
5. **Markets** — Anthropic prices its IPO at $83 a share, raising a
   record $81.3 billion at a ~$1.5 trillion valuation.
6. **Health** — Ebola in DR Congo passes 11,000 cases, the
   second-largest outbreak on record; the WHO keeps its emergency
   declaration in place.

The ticker adds three more: Cyclone Akvan nearing India, India–Japan
talks in Tokyo, and Ethiopia–Eritrea tensions.

Run it with: `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py`
